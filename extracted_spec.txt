THE CENTER should present as a credible, organized, approachable business and administrative support company. Visitors should understand within seconds who the company helps, what services are offered, what THE CENTER can and cannot do, and the next action to take.
Primary conversion: Book a Free Consultation.
Secondary conversion: Call 901-207-1660 or send a contact form inquiry.
Existing-client action: Make a Payment, separated from prospect-facing calls to action.
Resource goal: Provide helpful, current, plain-language information without implying legal, tax, immigration, or financial advice.
Brand promise: Start Here. Move Forward.

8 | PAGE SPECIFICATION - FORMS & OFFICIAL RESOURCES
Rename and reposition the current Tax Forms page
Recommended navigation label: Forms & Official Resources. The page may still contain business and tax forms, but it should emphasize education, source verification, and official agency links rather than looking like tax-preparation advice.
Resource-card structure
Form name and official form number.
What it is - 1-2 plain-language sentences.
Who typically uses/files it - use cautious wording such as "may" or "generally" where rules vary.
Timing - avoid oversimplified deadlines when facts depend on tax year, entity type, weekends/holidays, extensions, or other rules.
Official source button - IRS, USCIS, FinCEN, Tennessee Secretary of State, etc.
Last reviewed date - display prominently.
Short disclaimer: requirements change; verify with the issuing agency or qualified professional before filing.
2026 correction - Form 1099-NEC
Replace outdated $600 statement
For payments made in 2026, IRS guidance states that the reporting threshold for certain nonemployee compensation payments is $2,000. For payments before 2026, the prior threshold was $600; the threshold is subject to inflation adjustment after 2026. Avoid a universal statement because exceptions, including backup withholding, can apply.
2026 correction - Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI)
Replace current domestic-company wording
FinCEN states that entities created in the United States and their beneficial owners are exempt from BOI reporting under the current rule. Certain entities formed under foreign law and registered to do business in a U.S. state or tribal jurisdiction may still meet the reporting-company definition, subject to exemptions. Link directly to current FinCEN guidance and display a last-reviewed date.
